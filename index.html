8Bit Burn — Privacy Policy

Last updated 4 October 2026

8Bit Burn is a fitness challenge app you play with a small group — a “squad” — that you create or are invited into. This policy explains exactly what the app stores, who can see it, and how to delete it.

The short version. We store your gamer tag, your avatar, your weigh-ins, and daily activity totals so your squad can see your progress. Detailed health records never leave your phone. Nothing is sold, nothing is used for advertising, and you can delete everything from inside the app.

Who runs this app

8Bit Burn is an independent app made by Ramon Romero. There is no company behind it and no third party is given access to your data beyond the hosting provider described below.

What the app stores

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple, we receive an account identifier and, if you allow it, an email address. If you choose Apple's “Hide My Email”, we only ever see the relay address.
  • Your profile. Gamer tag, pixel avatar, and your device's time zone — the time zone is used so your daily challenge resets at your own midnight.
  • Your squads. Which squads you belong to and which season you are competing in.
  • Weigh-ins. The weight you log, and when. Only if you choose a weight-based goal.
  • Daily activity totals. One row per day: steps, active calories, exercise minutes, workout count, minutes in your heart-rate zone, and your average and resting heart rate for that day.
  • Game progress. Quests, how far along each one is, and the XP you have earned.

Health data

With your permission, 8Bit Burn reads step count, active energy, exercise minutes, workouts, heart rate and body weight from Apple Health. It writes back only the weigh-ins you log yourself.

Individual health samples never leave your device. Your phone reduces each day to the totals listed above and uploads only those. We never receive a minute-by-minute record of your heart rate or your movements.

Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with data brokers, and never disclosed to any third party for their own purposes. Granting Health access is optional — decline it and the app still works, with activity-based quests simply staying at zero.

Who can see your data

Members of a squad can see each other's gamer tag, avatar, progress toward the season goal, weigh-ins, daily activity totals and XP. That visibility is the point of a shared challenge, and you should only join squads you trust.

People outside your squad cannot see any of it. This is enforced at the database level, not just in the app.

If you belong to more than one squad, they are kept entirely separate. Members of one squad cannot see the other.

Where it is stored

Data is stored on Supabase, a hosting provider acting on our behalf. It is encrypted in transit and at rest. Supabase does not use your data for its own purposes.

Deleting your data

Open the app, go to the You tab and choose Delete my account. This permanently erases your profile, avatar, weigh-ins, activity totals, quests and XP, and the account itself. It cannot be undone and it happens immediately.

You can also leave an individual squad, which removes you and your results from that squad's season while leaving the rest of your account intact.

Deleting the app alone does not delete your data — use the in-app option first.

Young players

8Bit Burn is designed to be played by families. An account for anyone under 13 should be created and supervised by a parent or guardian.

Because tracking weight is not appropriate for every player, the app offers an activity goal: that player competes on exercise minutes instead, appears on the same scoreboard, earns the same XP, and is never asked for a weight.

What we do not do

  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or third-party tracking of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.
  • No location tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that affects what we collect or who can see it, the date at the top will change and the app will tell you.
